# ADR-0028: Defer scenario automation

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-07-14

## Context
Scenario automation was under consideration as a next piece of harness work. The
call to defer it was made and relayed in-session just before a standup; the fuller
rationale was not dictated. This record captures the decision now so it is not
lost or re-litigated informally — the write-up IS the note.

## Decision
Defer scenario automation: no scenario-automation piece is built for now.

## Consequences
- Scenario work stays manual until this is revisited; no guide, eval wiring, or
  checker rule is added for it in the meantime.
- The captured rationale is thin by design — the owner should flesh out Context
  when accepting this ADR, or supersede it if the reasoning turns out to differ.
- Revisit trigger: manual scenario work becoming a repeated drag, or scenario
  automation resurfacing as a planned piece of work.
